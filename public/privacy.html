Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Query Cart collects and how it is used. Query Cart is a student academic project for a Database Management Systems course, built for demonstration purposes only.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and a securely hashed password. Customers may also save delivery addresses.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to operate core platform features: logging in, placing orders, managing your shop (for sellers), and displaying your reviews and order history.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored in a local PostgreSQL database used solely for this academic project. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before saving.

4. Data Sharing

Your data is not shared with any third party. This project does not use external analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Cookies and Sessions

Query Cart uses a login token stored in your browser to keep you signed in. No tracking cookies are used.

6. Your Rights

You may update your profile information or change your password at any time from your account settings.

7. Contact

This project was built as part of a university coursework submission and is not intended for production or commercial use.
